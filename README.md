A Monday morning standup joke, told in one line. Entry for the make.fun bounty "Monday Morning Punchlines".

The joke riffs on standup culture, where everyone reports what they did, what they'll do, and what they're "blocked by" — a status tickets wear for weeks while waiting on someone else. Here the narrator files the whole weekend as blocked by Monday itself, then drags it into the backlog, that quiet graveyard where tasks are sent to be forgotten forever.

## Entry

> Monday morning standup: I reported my weekend as 'blocked by Monday' and dragged it into the backlog forever.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when `OUTPUT_DIR` is set).
